# HELLO, thanks for purchasing nex-loading from Nexeum Studios, here's a bit of information you may need for the script!

There's one main configuration area: script.js. This is located in ui/script.js. The area for configuration is clearly marked out, we do not advise changing anything outside that area, unless you know what you're doing. 

There's four main areas for configuration: Server Data, Backgrounds, Music and Themes:
- Server Data is nice and simple, just information about your server, like your discord information, socials, rules and team members. You MUST have 'Server Widget' enabled in your Discord Server (under Engagement Settings) for the Live Online Members count to work. 
- Backgrounds, there's two types of backgrounds: image, and video. These files MUST be in ui/assets/backgrounds to function and work as expected. OPTIMISE THESE, you don't want players downloading 100MB of videos or backgrounds, compression works!!!
- Music, stored in ui/assets/music is self-explanatory.
- THEMES, the cool stuff. The automated themes work off of dates, a start and an end. The default_theme will show when no theme is active. The icons are stored in ui/assets/header_icons. 

ANY QUESTIONS, reach out to our support thingy in our discord and we'll help you out!
